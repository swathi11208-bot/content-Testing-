Demo Video Plan (about 90 seconds)

  1. Introduce the project and your role: Content & Testing.
  2. Show the class selector and choose a class.
  3. Filter Mathematics and open a lesson.
  4. Show the explanation and answer a quiz question.
  5. Switch language to Tamil and show the equivalent lesson.
  6. Open an EVS/Science lesson from another class.
  7. Show the testing report, translation review and potential issue log.
  8. State clearly which tests are implemented and which need team integration or teacher review.

Suggested closing: “I prepared grade-wise sample learning content, bilingual examples, interactive quiz checks and a structured test report. Curriculum validation and integration testing will be completed with the full team.”
